# People Analytics report: user guide (1 page)

**Who it is for**: HR business partners and HR controlling.
**Data**: employees on the date you choose, refreshed monthly from the HR extract. Simulated data in this demo.

## How to use it
1. Pick the **date** in the top slicer (default: last day of the latest month).
2. Filter by **country** and **department** with the slicers on the left. All pages follow the same filters.
3. Read the cards first (headcount, FTE, labour cost, attrition, pay gap), then the charts.

## What the figures mean
- **Headcount**: people employed on the date. **FTE**: working time, two half-time employees = 1 FTE.
- **Labour cost**: yearly base pay times FTE plus bonus, plus employer social charges.
- **Attrition %**: leavers during the year divided by the average of headcount on 1 January and 31 December.
- **Gender pay gap %**: difference between average men and women base salary, divided by average men salary (apprentices excluded). Compare it within the same job level before drawing conclusions.

## Confidentiality
- Groups of fewer than 5 people are shown blank so no one can be identified.
- Do not export or share screenshots outside HR. Report access is limited to authorised HR users.

## Something looks wrong?
Check the date and filters first. If a figure still looks wrong, contact the HR analytics team with the page, filters and value you expected.
